Choosing a till

Six questions to ask any till. Ours included.

Hospitality people keep asking the same things. Here’s the list, with Hugo’s honest answer next to each.

  • 1Does the amount reach the card terminal on its own?Hugo: yes. Retyping amounts is how mistakes happen.
  • 2Can staff take orders at the table?Hugo: yes, from any phone, straight to the kitchen display.
  • 3Is stock included, or an add-on?Hugo: included, with recipes and stock-takes.
  • 4Shifts and staff?Hugo: included, unlimited staff.
  • 5Reports I can actually read?Hugo: live sales, plus an export for your accountant.
  • 6What does it cost in total — hardware, licence, add-ons?Hugo: 190 Kč a month and the phone you own. Full pricing.
