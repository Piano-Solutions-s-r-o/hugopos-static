Step-by-step guide

Tap to Pay on Android. Take cards on your phone.

Tap to Pay in the Hugo till turns a phone or tablet running Android 12 or newer, with NFC and Google Play, into a payment terminal. Activation is free and we approve it within 48 hours. You take cards once your business is verified too: the guest just holds a card, phone or watch to the back of the phone. The whole setup takes seven steps.

Before you start

What you will need

Nothing exotic. You probably have most of it in your pocket already.

  • ✓
    An Android phone or tablet running Android 12 or newerThe one that runs the Hugo till. It takes the cards itself.
  • ✓
    NFCThe contactless chip. Careful: some budget tablets lack it (the Samsung Galaxy Tab A9, for one).
  • ✓
    The Google Play StoreYou get both Hugo and Piano Payments from there.
  • ✓
    InternetWi-Fi or mobile data. The bank approves every card online, so it does not work offline.
  • ✓
    A Hugo accountNot yet? Create one for free, 14 days, no card needed.
  • ✓
    Your business verified for card paymentsID documents, a payout account and the contract. The account owner does this, see step 1.

Got an iPhone or iPad?

Tap to Pay on iPhone is on its way. Until then it takes cash, and cards through the Hugo terminal. Not sure about your Android? Type its model in while signing up to Hugo and it tells you whether it can do it.

Step by step

Seven steps to your first phone payment

Feel free to do steps 1 and 2 together: you can order Tap to Pay before your business is verified. Cards work only once both are done.

  1. 1
    Web Admin

    Verify your business for card payments

    Without it neither a terminal nor a phone takes cards; the law requires it. Have two ID documents and a Czech business account for payouts ready. You send a 1 CZK verification payment and sign the contract right in the Admin. Only the account owner sees this section.

    Admin › Card payments

  2. 2
    Web Admin

    Order Android Tap-to-Pay

    In the Admin Shop, add Android Tap-to-Pay to the cart and finish the order. It is free. Only the order starts the activation.

    Admin › Shop › Phone and tablets

    More than one phone? Order it once per device. Each activation code works only once.

    At checkout the Shop asks whether you accept meal vouchers. Answer truthfully; we arrange the contracts based on it.

  3. 3
    E-mail

    Wait for the e-mail

    Within 48 hours we approve and activate Tap to Pay. Then an e-mail arrives with everything you need: a link to the Piano Payments app, your activation code and the steps. Meanwhile, go ahead with step 5.

  4. Key step 4
    Phone

    Install Piano Payments and enter the code

    On the phone that will take cards, get the Piano Payments app from Google Play. Open it and enter the activation code from the e-mail. Done; just leave it on the home screen. Hugo opens it by itself for each payment.

    Piano Payments on Google Play
  5. 5
    Phone + Admin

    Install Hugo and pair the phone

    Already using this phone as a Hugo till? Skip this step. Otherwise get Hugo from Google Play and open it. It shows a pairing code. Enter it in the Admin and the phone is a till.

    Admin › Settings › Devices › Pair device

    Hugo on Google Play
  6. 6
    Web Admin

    Switch on phone payments

    In the Admin, find your Android till and tap Accept cards with Tap to Pay (Edit, once set up). If Hugo asks what kind of device it is, choose It's Android. Under Pay by phone, flip the switch on and save with Save Tap to Pay. Staff get signed out of the till, so sign in again with your PIN.

    Admin › Settings › Devices › your till › Accept cards with Tap to Pay

    You can flip the switch right away. Cards only work once Piano Payments is activated (step 4) and your business is verified (step 1). Leave the Advanced part closed.

  7. 7
    Phone

    Take your first card

    Ring up the bill and choose Card. Have a terminal on this till too? Pick Tap to Pay on this phone. The phone shows the amount and your guest holds the card to its back. Beep, paid.

What it looks like

Paid in three seconds. No cables, no boxes.

  1. You ring it upThe bill in Hugo as usual, then Card.
  2. The guest tapsA card, phone or watch. Against the back of the phone, where the NFC is.
  3. Beep, doneE-mail the receipt to your guest, or print it on a small Bluetooth printer.
When something is off

The usual snags

The Card button is grey and says Card after business verification (or after the 1 CZK payment or the contract)

Business verification, the 1 CZK payment or the contract is not finished yet. The owner completes it in the Admin under Card payments. Once it says everything is fine, Card appears on the till by itself. Until then, take cash.

Hugo asks me to install and activate Piano Payments

The Piano Payments app is missing on this phone, or the activation code has not been entered yet. Go back to step 4. No e-mail with the code within 48 hours? Check your spam, then write to us.

The payment ended saying Piano Payments did not complete it

Check that Piano Payments is activated and the phone is online, then try again. If Hugo reports an uncertain result of an earlier payment, do not charge the same bill again or another way. Follow the message on screen, or contact support.

I have two phones. Is one activation enough?

No. Each activation code works only once, so order Android Tap-to-Pay for every device. It is still free.

I also have a Hugo terminal. Do they work together?

Sure. One till can have both, and staff pick one at payment. The Hugo terminal needs neither Piano Payments nor the Tap to Pay activation. Business verification from step 1 applies to it too.

The guest wants a paper receipt

Get a small Bluetooth printer from the Shop in the Admin. How to connect it is in the guides on the How to start page.

I do not know whether my phone has NFC

Open Settings on the phone and search for NFC. Found it? Switch it on. Nothing there? The phone has no NFC and Tap to Pay will not work on it. You can also check the model while signing up to Hugo.
