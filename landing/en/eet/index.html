The exception: EET OFF

A sole trader in the first band of the flat-rate tax, with income up to 1 million Kč in the previous year? You can sign up for EET OFF and skip the reporting. The state charges an extra 1 400 Kč a month for that, though. That’s more than Hugo’s monthly subscription.

Your accountant will tell you whether you qualify and by when you have to sign up.

What gets reported

The state wants the total. Not what was on the plate.

Reported

  • Who sells (your company)
  • Where (venue and till)
  • When (date and time)
  • The receipt number
  • The total amount

Not reported

  • What was sold
  • VAT rates
  • Who paid
  • Invoices paid by transfer and online-shop payments

And you don’t have to print a receipt for EET.

No need to worry

What people worry about. And why they needn’t.

  • “Me and computers? No way.”

    Yes way. You download one file and upload it. If you can send a photo, you can do this.

  • “I don’t have time for this.”

    A few clicks with the state, a few with us. Hugo then does the sending and watches the certificate’s expiry too.

  • “What if I press the wrong thing?”

    Hugo tells you in plain words which step is still missing. No cryptic error codes.

  • “What if the state’s system is down?”

    Hugo holds the payment and sends it again on its own. You keep ringing up.
